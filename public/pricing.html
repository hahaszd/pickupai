What it costs you to miss the call instead

Most tradies miss calls while they're on the tools, driving, or under a house. The caller does not leave a voicemail — they ring the next result.

One recovered job a month usually covers the subscription several times over. That is the whole arithmetic, and it is worth doing with your own numbers rather than ours: take your average job value and ask how many calls a week go unanswered.

Compared with the alternatives

  • Voicemail — free, and most callers hang up rather than leave one. The job goes to whoever answered.
  • A part-time receptionist — several thousand a month, business hours only, and they take holidays. Better than PickupAI at knowing your business; not available at 2am.
  • A traditional call centre — usually per-call or per-minute, and the operator reads a generic script. They can't ask whether the water is still running.
  • Per-minute AI services — cheaper on a quiet month, more expensive exactly when you're busy.

Who this isn't for. If you already answer nearly every call, or you only want quoted work booked into a calendar with deposits taken, this isn't the right tool. It is for tradies whose phone rings when their hands are full.
